# Law Firm Google Ads Negative Keyword Starter List

Legal clicks are some of the most expensive in Google Ads, so every click from someone who will never hire the firm costs real money. Negative keywords stop ads from showing for those searches. This is a starter list, grouped by theme, to adapt before you add it to an account. Published by Legal Web Agency.

## How to use this list

1. Start from your own **search terms report**. The best negatives come from searches you have already paid for.
2. Add the themes below that clearly do not fit your firm. Some will be wrong for some practices (a firm that does free consultations should not block "free").
3. Use **phrase match** for most negatives, so the word blocks any search that contains it.
4. Put shared themes in a negative keyword list applied to every campaign; keep practice-specific negatives at campaign level.
5. Review the search terms report every week for the first month, then monthly.

## Jobs and careers

People looking for work, not legal help.

## Education and research

## Do-it-yourself and free help

Review these carefully; they depend on your practice and offer.

| Negative | Note |
|----------|------|
| forms, template, sample | Often DIY |
| legal aid, pro bono | Keep if you take pro bono work |
| free lawyer | Not "free consultation" |
| how to file without a lawyer | |

## Wrong practice or wrong side

| Negative | Note |
|----------|------|
| Practices you do not handle | e.g. "immigration" on a personal injury account |
| "for lawyers", "law firm software" | B2B searches from other firms |
| Opposing roles | e.g. "insurance adjuster jobs", "defense attorney" on a plaintiff account |

## Complaints and lookups

| Negative | Note |
|----------|------|
| competitor firm names | Unless you deliberately bid on them |
| reviews of [competitor], lawsuit against [competitor] | |
| bar complaint, disbarred | |

## What not to block

- **"Cost", "how much", "fees":** these are often people close to hiring.
- **"Free consultation":** keep, if you offer one.
- **Spanish-language searches:** block only if no one at the firm can take the call.
